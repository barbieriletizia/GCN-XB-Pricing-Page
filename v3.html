Frequently asked questions.

Learn more about pricing and entity country.

Why does the Stripe account country matter?

The account country is your entity country. It determines standard pricing, which products you can activate, payout destination, and some compliance requirements. It is separate from the language of stripe.com and from where your customers are located.

Can I change country after I sign up?

Changing country later usually means a new Stripe account and a new activation. Compare before you create an account so you are not choosing from the footer country selector after the fact.

Do I need a company in that country?

You generally need a business that can be verified in the country you select, including a bank account Stripe can pay out to. Requirements vary. If you are incorporating a US company from abroad, Atlas is built for that path.

How are availability gaps shown?

If a product is offered, you see a check and “Included,” or the local price. If it is not offered in that country, you see an em dash—not a hidden footnote. Limited or preview capabilities use a Limited label.

How much does Stripe cost?

Transaction pricing with Stripe depends on the account country, the payment method, and the products you use. Compare countries.
